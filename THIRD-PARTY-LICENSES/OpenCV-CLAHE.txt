OpenCV CLAHE adapted source notice and provenance
=================================================

Lumora includes a U16-only sequential prepared CLAHE implementation adapted
from OpenCV 4.12.0, vcpkg port-version 9:

https://github.com/opencv/opencv/blob/4.12.0/modules/imgproc/src/clahe.cpp

Local inspected source path:
modules/imgproc/src/clahe.cpp

Adaptation scope: fixed owned LUT, histogram, interpolation and reflection
arrays; direct reflected byte access for U16 images; no OpenCV scheduler or
backend dispatch; pinned OpenCV LUT, interpolation and rounding arithmetic.
Lumora continues to use OpenCV's inline scalar saturate_cast helper.
